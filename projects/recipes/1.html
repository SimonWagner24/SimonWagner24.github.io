Fluffy Pancakes

Prep time: 5 mins

Cook time: 20 mins

Total time: 25 mins

Servings: 12 people

Why I Chose this Recipe

When I was in elementary school, me, my sister, and my dad loved to make these fluffy pancakes all the time for breakfast on the weekend.My dad said that The 'buttermilk' created from the vinigar and milk mixture creates a more fluffy texture.

Ingredients Amount
All-purpose flour 1 Cup
Granulated suger 1/4 Cup
Baking powder 4 tsp
Baking soda 1/4 tsp
Salt 1/2 tsp
Milk 1 3/4 Cups
Butter 1/4 Cup
Vanilla extract 2 tsp
Large egg 1

Instructions

  1. Combine all of the dry ingredients including the flour, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt into a mixing bowl and add a well in the center to pour in the wet mixture in later.
  2. Use a wire whisk to combine the milk, melted butter, vanilla, and egg first, then slowly fold them into the dry ingredients. Mix together until smooth (there may be a couple of lumps but that's okay).
  3. Set the batter aside and allow to rest while heating up your pan or griddle.
  4. Heat a nonstick pan or griddle over low-medium heat and wipe over with a little butter to lightly grease pan. Pour ¼ cup of batter onto the pan and spread out gently into a round shape with the back of your ladle or measuring cup.
  5. When the underside is golden and bubbles begin to appear on the surface, flip with a spatula and cook until golden.
  6. Serve however you like. You can use honey, maple syrup, fruit of your choice, or any other ways you would like to eat your pancakes.
